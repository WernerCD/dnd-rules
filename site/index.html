About

A quick reference for the rules that come up most at the table, with the rules text for the 2014 rules (System Reference Document 5.1) and the 2024 rules (System Reference Document 5.2.1). Pick your rules edition at the top; where the two editions differ, each topic says what changed, and Compare editions shows both texts side by side.

Every topic has its own link (the # button copies it), so you can send someone straight to, say, grapple and shove or Exhaustion. To print a cheat sheet, filter or search, expand the topics you want in full, and press Print: collapsed topics print as their one-line summary.

The rules text marked SRD 5.1 or SRD 5.2.1 comes from the System Reference Documents 5.1 and 5.2.1 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at dnd.wizards.com and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. The 2014 text was taken from the 5e-bits database and the 2024 text from a Markdown conversion of SRD 5.2.1. The one-line summaries and the "what changed" notes are original to this site.
