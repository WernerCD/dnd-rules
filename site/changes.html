Changes

What has changed on the Rules Reference, newest first. Back to the Rules Reference.

November 19, 2026

  • Dice rolling. Dice in the rules text, such as the 1d6 per 10 feet for falling, can be tapped to roll them. The roll tray keeps a log shared with the other D&D Toolbox tools.
  • Links to the other tools. Spell and magic item names in the rules text now open in the Spell Planner and the Item Finder, and the other tools link conditions straight to this site.
  • Works offline. After one visit the Rules Reference opens with no signal, and you can add it to your phone's home screen like an app.
  • Search from the hub. The D&D Toolbox's "Search every tool" box now finds every rule topic and condition here, and the link opens that topic.
  • Preview cards. Links to the site shared in chats and posts now show a picture card with its name and icon.
  • Search fix. Clearing the search box and then quickly picking a category no longer brings the old search back.
  • Tested before every update. Each update to the site is now checked in a real browser, on a computer screen and a phone screen, before it goes live.

November 18, 2026

  • Condition links. Links like #condition-poisoned open that condition, so other D&D Toolbox pages can link straight to it. Short links such as #poisoned still work.
  • Launch. A quick rules reference for the table, for the 2014 rules (SRD 5.1) and the 2024 rules (SRD 5.2.1): all 15 conditions, the actions, bonus actions, reactions, opportunity attacks, two-weapon fighting, grapple and shove, cover, mounted combat, resistance and vulnerability, dropping to 0 HP and death saves, temporary hit points, resting, concentration, the bonus action spell rule, spell components, vision and light, hiding, travel pace, falling and suffocating. Each topic has a one-line summary, its own link, and a note on what changed in 2024, and Compare editions shows both texts side by side. Search, category filters, a topic list on wide screens, and a print layout.
